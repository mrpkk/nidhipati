What it proves

Each finding is tied to a row and a rule; the same input produces the same output; the three amounts are separated; a missing price or unknown asset is reported rather than guessed.

What it does not prove

It is not a statutory audit, not a legal claim, and not a recovery. It does not verify the chain in v0.2. Missing rows cannot be found. Confirm each finding against the provider invoice before acting.
