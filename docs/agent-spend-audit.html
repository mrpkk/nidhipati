Eight checks before the invoice

  • Duplicate retries without idempotency
  • Quote vs settled price
  • Failed call that still billed
  • Runaway loop / retry storm
  • Missing refund after over-authorization
  • Wrong asset, network or payee
  • Frontier model on a simple task
  • Budget that lives in the prompt, not the signing path

What you get

A written report: finding, evidence, estimated overpay, fix. We mark estimates as estimates; “recovered” means verified against the provider invoice or transaction.
