The $5,000 finding guarantee

If the paid audit does not identify at least $5,000 in documented overcharges or refund opportunities from the records and invoices you provide within 10 working days, we refund the audit fee.

"Documented" means a row number, a receipt/invoice or transaction hash, and a written basis. Estimates do not count. This is about identified amounts, not what a provider actually returns.

What is included

Written report, row-level evidence, amounts kept separate, what could not be checked, and a fix list. One retest of agreed findings.

What is not included

No legal, tax, or investment advice. No guarantee of recovery: the finding guarantee is about identified amounts, not what providers return. No custody or money movement. No statutory audit opinion. If a charge is correct, it stays correct.
