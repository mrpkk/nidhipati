Proof of reserve is not a statutory audit

A small RWA issuer needs a verifiable report: what backs the token, how NAV is calculated, which sources were used, and when. Nidhipati signs the evidence; a licensed auditor/legal partner handles the regulated opinion.

  • Reserve composition and custody
  • Bank/custodian statement hash
  • NAV inputs and formulas
  • Price sources and timestamps
  • Outstanding token supply
  • Reconciliation and exceptions
  • Signed report and evidence links
  • Human/legal review

Who it is for

Small RWA issuers, funds, DAOs and treasuries that need an independent evidence report without building a full audit team.

Data we can use

RWA.xyz, Chainstack rwa-sdk, RedStone, custodian statements, on-chain supply, issuer NAV files. If a source is missing, the report says “missing” — it does not invent a number.
