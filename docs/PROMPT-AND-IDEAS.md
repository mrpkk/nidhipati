## 2. Product thesis

- AI agents got wallets (LLM/API, MCP, x402, USDC, stablecoin, agentic commerce) but not **proof**.
- A dashboard shows **how much**; it does not prove **what for**.
- The gap: independent cross-rail **audit + receipt + recovery**.
- Product: **Nidhipati** — not a rail, not a dashboard, not a token, not custody.
- Promise: «Рельсы двигают деньги. Nidhipati доказывает, что движение было правильным.»

## 5. Decisions

1. **Do not build another payment rail/OS** — 112 agent-payment startups, $115M 2026; crowded.
2. **Do not build a dashboard** — FinOps/observability crowded; wedge is proof/recovery.
3. **Proof validator first** — 1–2 weeks; smaller than full OS.
4. **RWA:** attestation/proof-of-reserve/NAV, not issuance.
5. **Stablecoin:** reconcile/invoice/ops, not issuance/custody.
6. **Prediction:** resolution evidence/data, not market making.
7. **SEO:** task first, brand second; no ads until revenue.
8. **No calls; no lies; no budget.**
9. **One unique feature:** Proof of Spend — cross-rail signed receipt.
