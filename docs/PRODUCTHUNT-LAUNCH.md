## First comment (post immediately after launch)

> I built Nidhipati after watching an agent bill where every line looked plausible and none of it could be reconciled to a written permission.
>
> The free part is a local audit: paste a CSV/JSON of charges; it flags missing mandate, amount above cap, duplicate idempotency keys, failed-but-billed calls, missing refunds, unknown asset/network, runaway loops. Nothing is uploaded; no account; if it finds nothing, it says so.
>
> The paid part is a written audit with row numbers, provider reconciliation and a recovery pack. For tokenized assets there is a signed reserve/NAV attestation that a buyer can verify offline.
>
> I am looking for one team to run the free scan on a real month of agent spend and tell me which findings are noise. That is more useful than another dashboard.
>
> Free scan: https://mrpkk.github.io/nidhipati/free-spend-audit.html
> Repo: https://github.com/mrpkk/nidhipati
