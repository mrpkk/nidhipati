Ten checks before you pay the agent bill

  • Duplicate retries — same tx hash, or equal payment within 120s without a receipt on both rows.
  • Price escalation — settled above quote by more than 1%.
  • Failed-but-billed — status failed/error/timeout/5xx while settled > 0.
  • Runaway loop — more than 30 calls in any 60s window for one agent.
  • Missing refund — charged more than actual, refund not recorded.
  • Unknown asset/network — the row does not say where the money went.
  • Missing payee — no recipient recorded.
  • Model overpay — above 3× the same agent’s median for the same tool/model.
  • Budget bypass — no policy file; the budget lives in a prompt.
  • Budget exceeded — settled above the per-agent limit.

Run the same checks yourself: nidhipati-spend audit your.csv. If the tool answers your question, you do not need us.

What a report looks like

Every finding has a row number, the rule, the amount, and the evidence. Three amounts stay separate: money without a reason, above the usual price, over the budget. Adding them into one “saved” number would be dishonest.
